❤️ Funding openmaps.fr

Openmaps.fr (See presentation page) Tile Map Service based on OpenStreetMap datas providing OpenTopoMap-R and OpenHikingMap All free of charge and Open Sourced code. However, operating and renting servers is not free and entirely rely on users donations. there are two crowdfunding platforms to help fund the project:

  • ko-fi.com/openmapsfr: 1.5% fee + 0.25 cents and allows for one-time donations as well as recurring ones. Preferred.
  • liberapay.com/openmaps.fr: 1.5% fee + 0.25 cents and only allows for recurring donations (unless you cancel the donation later). However, it is based on free software code.

Please note: These two platforms will ask for an email address. You can perfectly well provide a “dummy” email that does not exist, such as a@gmail.com, without this causing any issue if you prefer to remain anonymous.
